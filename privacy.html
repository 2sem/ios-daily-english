Privacy Policy

iOS Daily English · Last updated: 2026-10-11

"iOS Daily English" is a personal tool used by its owner to prepare study cards and to upload and schedule the owner's own videos on the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts.

What the app accesses

With the owner's explicit consent (Google sign-in), the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload a video, set its thumbnail and schedule its publication on the channel the owner signs in with.

What is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. The app does not collect, store on any server, sell, share or transfer personal data, and does not use Google user data for advertising.

Card generator

The card generator page on this site runs entirely in your browser. The text you paste is not sent anywhere.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
