📱 iOS Daily English - Day 176

ModelContainer
- An object that sets up where your SwiftData models are stored.
- Example: "Create one ModelContainer for the whole app."

Query
- A property wrapper that fetches saved models and keeps the view up to date.
- Example: "Use Query to load all notes in the list."

ModelContext
- The workspace where you insert, change and delete SwiftData models.
- Example: "Insert the new note into the ModelContext."
