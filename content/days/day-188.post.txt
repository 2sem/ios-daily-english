📱 iOS Daily English - Day 188

AsyncImage
- A view that downloads an image from a web address and shows it.
- Example: "Use AsyncImage for the profile picture."

SymbolRenderingMode
- A setting that chooses how an SF Symbol uses color, such as multicolor or hierarchical.
- Example: "Set the SymbolRenderingMode to multicolor."

ContentTransition
- A setting that decides how the content of a view changes, such as rolling digits.
- Example: "Add a ContentTransition to the like counter."
