📱 iOS Daily English - Day 230

BGTaskScheduler
- The system service where your app asks for time to run background work later.
- Example: "Register the refresh task with BGTaskScheduler at launch."

BGAppRefreshTask
- A short background task that lets your app fetch fresh data while it is not on screen.
- Example: "The BGAppRefreshTask updates the feed overnight."

Expiration Handler
- A block of code that the system calls when time is almost up, so you can stop work cleanly.
- Example: "Cancel the download in the expiration handler."
