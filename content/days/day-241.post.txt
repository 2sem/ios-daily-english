📱 iOS Daily English - Day 241

Non-Fatal Error
- An error that your app catches and reports, while the app keeps running.
- Example: "Report every non-fatal error with context."

Breadcrumb
- A short note about a user step, saved before an error, so you can see what led to it.
- Example: "Add a breadcrumb for each screen the user opens."

Crash-Free Rate
- The share of users or sessions that did not hit a crash, shown as a percentage.
- Example: "Our crash-free rate is ninety-nine percent."
