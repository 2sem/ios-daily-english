📱 iOS Daily English - Day 261

Task Cancellation
- A request that tells a task to stop its work as soon as it can.
- Example: "Task cancellation tells the work to stop."

CancellationError
- An error that Swift throws to show that a task was cancelled.
- Example: "Catch CancellationError and stop quietly."

Cancellation Handler
- A closure that runs right away when a task is cancelled, even while the task is waiting.
- Example: "Add a cancellation handler to close the connection."
