📱 iOS Daily English - Day 171

Stepper
- A control with minus and plus buttons that changes a number up or down.
- Example: "Use a Stepper for the number of tickets."

DatePicker
- A control that lets people choose a date, a time or both.
- Example: "Add a DatePicker so users can pick the trip day."

ColorPicker
- A control that opens a color panel so people can choose a color.
- Example: "A ColorPicker saves the theme color the user chose."
