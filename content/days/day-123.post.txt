📱 iOS Daily English - Day 123

Haptic Feedback
- A small vibration from the device that you feel when you touch the screen.
- Example: "Add Haptic Feedback to the like button."

Feedback Generator
- An object that creates haptic feedback and can get ready ahead of time.
- Example: "Create the Feedback Generator once, then call prepare before the tap."

Impact Style
- The strength of a tap vibration, such as light, medium or heavy.
- Example: "Pick a light Impact Style for small buttons."
