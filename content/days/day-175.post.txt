📱 iOS Daily English - Day 175

Table
- A view that shows rows of data in sortable columns.
- Example: "Use a Table to show the team on iPad and Mac."

TableColumn
- One column of a Table that has a title and a value for each row.
- Example: "Add a TableColumn for the name."

OutlineGroup
- A view that shows tree data as rows you can open and close.
- Example: "Use an OutlineGroup to show folders and files."
