📱 iOS Daily English - Day 178

contentMargins
- A modifier that adds space around the content of a scroll view.
- Example: "Add contentMargins so the first photo has space."

scrollTargetBehavior
- A modifier that tells a scroll view where to stop, such as one page at a time.
- Example: "Set scrollTargetBehavior to paging for the carousel."

scrollTargetLayout
- A modifier that marks the views inside a container as places where scrolling can stop.
- Example: "Add scrollTargetLayout to the stack of photos."
