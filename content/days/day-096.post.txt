📱 iOS Daily English - Day 96

Scroll View
- A view that lets the user drag to see content that is bigger than the screen.
- Example: "Put the long form inside a Scroll View, so it works on small phones."

Content Size
- The full width and height of everything inside a scroll view, including the part you can't see.
- Example: "It won't scroll because the Content Size is still zero."

Content Offset
- The point that shows how far a scroll view has moved from its starting position.
- Example: "Check the Content Offset to see if the user reached the bottom."
