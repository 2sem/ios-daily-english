📱 iOS Daily English - Day 180

visualEffect
- A modifier that changes how a view looks based on its position, without changing its layout.
- Example: "Use visualEffect to blur cards near the edge."

scrollTransition
- A modifier that changes a view while it scrolls into or out of the visible area.
- Example: "Add scrollTransition so the cards fade at the edges."

Custom Transition
- Your own type that decides how a view looks as it appears and disappears.
- Example: "Write a Custom Transition for the popup."
