📱 iOS Daily English - Day 179

GridRow
- A row inside a Grid that places its views in columns.
- Example: "Put each pair of labels in a GridRow."

safeAreaInset
- A modifier that adds a view at an edge and moves the content out of its way.
- Example: "Add the player bar with safeAreaInset."

containerRelativeFrame
- A modifier that sizes a view as a share of its container, such as a scroll view.
- Example: "Use containerRelativeFrame to make each card 80 percent wide."
