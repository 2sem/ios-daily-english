📱 iOS Daily English - Day 102

Main Thread
- The thread that draws your screen and handles touches, so it must stay free.
- Example: "Never block the Main Thread with a network call."

Dispatch Queue
- A line of tasks that the system runs for you on threads it manages.
- Example: "Send the image resize to a background Dispatch Queue."

Quality of Service
- A level that tells the system how important and urgent a task is.
- Example: "Use a low Quality of Service for the background cleanup."
