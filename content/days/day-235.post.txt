📱 iOS Daily English - Day 235

Feature Flag
- A setting that turns a feature on or off without shipping a new version of the app.
- Example: "Hide the new tab behind a feature flag."

Kill Switch
- A remote setting that lets you turn a broken feature off right away for every user.
- Example: "Flip the kill switch, checkout is crashing."

Gradual Rollout
- A release plan that shows a new feature to a small share of users first, then to more over time.
- Example: "Start the gradual rollout at ten percent."
