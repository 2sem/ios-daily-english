📱 iOS Daily English - Day 181

symbolEffect
- A modifier that animates an SF Symbol, for example with a bounce or a pulse.
- Example: "Add symbolEffect so the bell bounces."

sensoryFeedback
- A modifier that plays a haptic or a sound when a value changes.
- Example: "Use sensoryFeedback to vibrate after saving."

MeshGradient
- A view that blends colors smoothly across a grid of points.
- Example: "Use a MeshGradient for the login background."
