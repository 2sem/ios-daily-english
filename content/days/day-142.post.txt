📱 iOS Daily English - Day 142

Navigation Title
- The title that a screen shows in the navigation bar.
- Example: "Set the Navigation Title to Notes."

Toolbar
- The bar area where you place buttons for a screen.
- Example: "Put the add button in the Toolbar."

Navigation Destination
- A modifier that tells the stack which screen to show for a type of value.
- Example: "Add a Navigation Destination for the note type."
