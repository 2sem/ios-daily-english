📱 iOS Daily English - Day 177

ProposedViewSize
- The size a parent suggests to a view, which the view can accept or ignore.
- Example: "The layout reads the ProposedViewSize first."

LayoutSubviews
- The collection of child views that a custom layout measures and places.
- Example: "Loop over the LayoutSubviews to place each tag."

Layout Protocol
- A protocol for a custom container that measures its children and places them.
- Example: "Write a Layout Protocol type for the tag row."
