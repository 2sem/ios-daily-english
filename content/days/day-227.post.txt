📱 iOS Daily English - Day 227

Reachability
- Whether the device can reach the network right now.
- Example: "Check reachability before you upload the photo."

NWPathMonitor
- An Apple class that watches the network and tells your app when the connection changes.
- Example: "Start an NWPathMonitor when the app launches."

Network Path
- A snapshot of the current network connection, such as its status and whether it is expensive.
- Example: "The network path says the phone is on cellular."
