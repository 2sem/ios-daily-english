📱 iOS Daily English - Day 165

Chart
- A view from the Charts framework that draws your data as marks.
- Example: "Put the steps into a Chart on the home screen."

BarMark
- A mark that draws one bar for a value, for example steps per day.
- Example: "Use a BarMark for each day."

LineMark
- A mark that connects values with a line, to show a trend over time.
- Example: "Switch to a LineMark to show the trend."
