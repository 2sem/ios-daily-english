📱 iOS Daily English - Day 145

TabView
- A container that shows one area at a time and lets the user switch with a bar.
- Example: "Use a TabView for Home, Search and Profile."

Tab Item
- The icon and the title that a child view shows in the tab bar.
- Example: "Add a Tab Item with a house icon."

Tab Selection
- The value that decides which tab is shown.
- Example: "Store the Tab Selection in State, so you can switch tabs from code."
