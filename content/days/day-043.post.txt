📱 iOS Daily English - Day 43

KeyPath
- A value that points to a property of a type, so you can read that property later.
- Example: "Pass the KeyPath `\.title` to `map`, and you get every song title."

WritableKeyPath
- A key path that can also change the property it points to on a mutable value.
- Example: "It's a WritableKeyPath, so the form can update any field."

Dynamic Member Lookup
- An attribute that lets a type answer dot-style member access through one subscript you write.
- Example: "With Dynamic Member Lookup, `Box` shows the pet's `name` directly."
