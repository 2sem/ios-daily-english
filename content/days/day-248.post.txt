📱 iOS Daily English - Day 248

Notification Payload
- The data inside a push message, such as the alert text, the badge number and your own values.
- Example: "Put the chat id in the notification payload."

UNUserNotificationCenter
- The system object your app uses to ask permission for, schedule and handle notifications.
- Example: "UNUserNotificationCenter calls your delegate on a tap."

Silent Push
- A push message with no alert that wakes the app in the background to fetch new data.
- Example: "A silent push can wake the app to fetch data."
