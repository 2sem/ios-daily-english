📱 iOS Daily English - Day 156

Shape
- A protocol for a form that draws itself inside the space it is given.
- Example: "Make the badge a custom Shape, so we can reuse it."

Stroke
- A line drawn along the edge of a shape, instead of filling the inside.
- Example: "Add a Stroke, because the design only has an outline."

Path
- A list of lines and curves that you build step by step to draw a custom form.
- Example: "Build the arrow as a Path, with three lines."
