📱 iOS Daily English - Day 86

Stack View
- A view that lines up its subviews in a row or a column and creates their layout rules for you.
- Example: "Put the three buttons in a Stack View, then you only need constraints for the stack."

Safe Area
- The part of the screen not covered by the notch, the home indicator or system bars, where content is safe to show.
- Example: "Pin the toolbar to the Safe Area, or it hides under the home indicator."

Layout Margins
- The padding inside a view's edges that other views can line up against.
- Example: "Use the Layout Margins, so every card has the same inner padding."
