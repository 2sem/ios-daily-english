📱 iOS Daily English - Day 143

Sheet
- A screen that slides up over the current one, and the user can swipe it down.
- Example: "Show the note editor in a Sheet."

Full Screen Cover
- A screen that slides up and covers the whole screen, with no swipe to close.
- Example: "Use a Full Screen Cover for the login screen."

Presentation Detent
- A height that a sheet can stop at, such as medium or large.
- Example: "Add a Presentation Detent, so the sheet opens at half height."
