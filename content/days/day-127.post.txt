📱 iOS Daily English - Day 127

Share Sheet
- The panel that slides up so the user can send content to another app.
- Example: "Tap Share to open the Share Sheet."

Activity Item
- A piece of content, such as text or a link, that you want to share.
- Example: "Add the note text and its link as Activity Items."

Activity View Controller
- A built-in view controller that shows the share sheet with your items.
- Example: "Create an Activity View Controller and present it from the button action."
