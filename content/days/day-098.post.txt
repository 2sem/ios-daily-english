📱 iOS Daily English - Day 98

Storyboard
- A visual file where you design screens and link them together by dragging.
- Example: "Open the Storyboard, the login screen is the first one."

Outlet
- A property that connects an object in the storyboard to your code.
- Example: "The label is nil because the Outlet isn't connected."

Segue
- A transition from one screen to another that you define in a storyboard.
- Example: "Use a Segue to open the detail screen, then pass the data in `prepare`."
