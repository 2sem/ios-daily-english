📱 iOS Daily English - Day 286

Load on Appear
- Starting a view's data loading at the moment the view shows on screen.
- Example: "Do the load on appear, not in the initializer."

Automatic Cancellation
- The way SwiftUI cancels a view's task by itself when the view goes away.
- Example: "Automatic cancellation stops the request when the user leaves."

task(id:)
- A SwiftUI modifier that restarts its task whenever the given value changes.
- Example: "Use `task(id:)` to reload when the filter changes."
