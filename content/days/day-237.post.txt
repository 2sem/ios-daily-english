📱 iOS Daily English - Day 237

Logger
- The Apple type from the os framework that writes messages to the system log.
- Example: "Replace print with Logger in the network layer."

Log Level
- A label such as debug, info or error that shows how important a log message is.
- Example: "The log level hides debug messages in release."

Subsystem
- A name, usually your bundle identifier, that groups the log messages from one part of your app.
- Example: "Use the bundle identifier as the subsystem."
