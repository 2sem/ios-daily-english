📱 iOS Daily English - Day 209

Keychain
- A secure storage system on the device for small secrets like passwords and tokens.
- Example: "Store the refresh token in the keychain."

SecItemAdd
- The function that saves a new item, such as a password, into the keychain.
- Example: "Call SecItemAdd to save the token."

SecItemCopyMatching
- The function that searches the keychain and returns the item that matches your query.
- Example: "Use SecItemCopyMatching to read the saved token."
