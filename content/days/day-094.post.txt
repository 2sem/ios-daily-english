📱 iOS Daily English - Day 94

Local Notification
- An alert your app schedules on the device itself, for a time or a place, with no server involved.
- Example: "The workout reminder is a Local Notification, it works in airplane mode."

Push Notification
- An alert your server sends through Apple's push service to a user's device.
- Example: "The chat message arrives as a Push Notification from our server."

Notification Center
- In code, the `NotificationCenter` object that broadcasts messages between parts of your app; users never see these.
- Example: "Post to Notification Center when the cart changes, so the badge updates."
