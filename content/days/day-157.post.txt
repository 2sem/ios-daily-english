📱 iOS Daily English - Day 157

Shadow
- A soft dark copy behind a view that makes it look lifted from the screen.
- Example: "Add a Shadow, so the card stands out."

LinearGradient
- A color effect that changes smoothly from one color to another along a straight line.
- Example: "Use a LinearGradient from blue to purple for the header."

Material
- A blurred, see-through background that lets the content behind it shine through.
- Example: "Put the controls on a Material, so the photo stays visible."
