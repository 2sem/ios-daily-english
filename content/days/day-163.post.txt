📱 iOS Daily English - Day 163

Menu
- A button that opens a small list of actions when the user taps it.
- Example: "Put the three sort buttons into a Menu."

DisclosureGroup
- A container that the user can open and close to show or hide its content.
- Example: "Use a DisclosureGroup for the advanced filters."

ContentUnavailableView
- A view that explains why a screen is empty, for example when a search finds nothing.
- Example: "Show a ContentUnavailableView when the list is empty."
