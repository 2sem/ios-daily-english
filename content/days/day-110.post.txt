📱 iOS Daily English - Day 110

Activity Indicator
- A small spinning wheel that shows the app is busy.
- Example: "Show an Activity Indicator while the notes load."

Pull to Refresh
- A gesture where the user drags a list down to load fresh data.
- Example: "Pull to Refresh is the usual way to reload a feed."

Refresh Control
- A built-in control that you attach to a table to start a refresh and show a spinner.
- Example: "Attach a Refresh Control to the table, then call `endRefreshing` when the data arrives."
