📱 iOS Daily English - Day 173

LabeledContent
- A view that shows a label on one side and a value on the other.
- Example: "Show the app version in a LabeledContent row."

GroupBox
- A container that puts related content in a box with a title.
- Example: "Put the account details in a GroupBox."

ControlGroup
- A container that joins related controls, such as buttons, into one set.
- Example: "Place undo and redo in a ControlGroup."
