📱 iOS Daily English - Day 245

Group Identifier
- A name that starts with group and is the same in the app and its extensions, so they can share data.
- Example: "Use the same group identifier in the widget."

Shared Container
- A folder on disk that the app and its extensions can all read and write.
- Example: "Both targets save the file in the shared container."

Shared Defaults
- Settings saved in a UserDefaults suite that the app and its widget can both read.
- Example: "Save the unread count in shared defaults."
