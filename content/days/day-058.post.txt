📱 iOS Daily English - Day 58

Integer Overflow
- When a result is too big or too small for its integer type, so Swift stops the app with a crash.
- Example: "The badge count hit Integer Overflow because it was a `UInt8`."

Overflow Operator
- An operator like `&+` that wraps around to the other end of the range instead of crashing.
- Example: "Use an Overflow Operator only when wrapping around is what you want."

FixedWidthInteger
- The protocol for integer types with a set number of bits, giving them `bitWidth`, `max` and overflow-checking methods.
- Example: "Make the helper generic over FixedWidthInteger, so it works for every integer size."
