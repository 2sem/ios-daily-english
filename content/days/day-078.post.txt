📱 iOS Daily English - Day 78

Whitespace
- Characters you can't see, like spaces, tabs and newlines, that often sneak into user input.
- Example: "The login failed because the email had Whitespace at the end."

CharacterSet
- A Foundation type that describes a group of characters, often used to trim them from the ends of a string.
- Example: "Pass a CharacterSet with the hash sign, so tags come back without it."

Case Insensitive Comparison
- Comparing two strings while ignoring the difference between capital and small letters.
- Example: "Usernames need a Case Insensitive Comparison, or `Mina` and `mina` both get in."
