📱 iOS Daily English - Day 192

Business Logic
- The rules of your app that stay the same no matter how the screen looks.
- Example: "Keep the business logic out of the view controller."

Separation of Concerns
- A design idea where each part of the code has one clear job.
- Example: "Separation of concerns makes this module easy to read."

Presentation Logic
- The code that decides how data looks on the screen, like text and format.
- Example: "The price format is presentation logic, so it goes in the view model."
