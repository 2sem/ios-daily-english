📱 iOS Daily English - Day 184

TimelineEntry
- A protocol for the data of a widget at one moment in time, with a date.
- Example: "Add the streak count to the TimelineEntry."

WidgetFamily
- An enum for the sizes of a widget, such as small, medium and large.
- Example: "Check the WidgetFamily before you choose the layout."

Timeline
- A list of widget entries with dates, and a rule for when to ask for more.
- Example: "Return a Timeline with one entry per hour."
