📱 iOS Daily English - Day 26

Sorted
- A collection method that returns a new array with the items put in order.
- Example: "Use sorted to show the highest scores first."

CompactMap
- A collection method that changes every item and drops the results that are nil.
- Example: "Use compactMap to turn the text fields into numbers and skip the bad ones."

FlatMap
- A collection method that changes every item into a list and joins all the lists into one flat array.
- Example: "Use flatMap to join every team's members into one list."
