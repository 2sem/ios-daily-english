📱 iOS Daily English - Day 101

Subview
- A view that sits inside another view.
- Example: "Add the label as a Subview of the card."

Superview
- The view that directly contains another view.
- Example: "If the Superview is nil, the view isn't on screen yet."

View Hierarchy
- The tree of views, where every view sits inside a parent and can hold its own children.
- Example: "Open the View Hierarchy debugger to see which view covers the button."
