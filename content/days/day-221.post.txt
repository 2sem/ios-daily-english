📱 iOS Daily English - Day 221

CloudKit
- An Apple framework that stores app data in iCloud, so it can sync across the user's devices.
- Example: "We use CloudKit to share notes between the iPhone and the iPad."

CKContainer
- An object that stands for your app's iCloud storage and gives you access to its databases.
- Example: "Open the CKContainer with the identifier from the capability."

CKRecord
- One item stored in iCloud, like a row with named fields.
- Example: "Each note is a CKRecord with a title field."
