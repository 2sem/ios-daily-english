📱 iOS Daily English - Day 144

Alert
- A small window that shows a short message and waits for the answer.
- Example: "Show an Alert before the app deletes the note."

Confirmation Dialog
- A list of choices that slides up from the bottom to confirm an action.
- Example: "Use a Confirmation Dialog for Delete, Archive and Cancel."

Popover
- A small floating panel that points to the view that opened it.
- Example: "Show the help text in a Popover."
