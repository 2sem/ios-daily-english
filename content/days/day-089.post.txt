📱 iOS Daily English - Day 89

Navigation Controller
- A container that manages a stack of screens and shows a bar with a back button.
- Example: "Wrap the list in a Navigation Controller, then the title bar comes for free."

Push Navigation
- Moving to a new screen that slides in from the side and is added to the stack, with a back button.
- Example: "Use Push Navigation for the detail screen, users expect a back button there."

Modal Presentation
- Showing a screen on top of the current one, usually sliding up, for a separate task.
- Example: "Settings opens as a Modal Presentation, with a Done button at the top."
