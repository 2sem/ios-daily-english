📱 iOS Daily English - Day 104

Layer
- The drawing surface behind every view, where effects like shadows and rounded corners live.
- Example: "Set the shadow on the Layer, not on the view."

Corner Radius
- The size of the curve that rounds the corners of a layer.
- Example: "A Corner Radius of twelve makes the card look friendly."

Clips To Bounds
- A setting that hides any content that goes outside a view's edges.
- Example: "The image still has square corners, so turn on Clips To Bounds."
