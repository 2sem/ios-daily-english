📱 iOS Daily English - Day 189

keyboardShortcut
- A modifier that lets people trigger a button with keys on a keyboard.
- Example: "Add keyboardShortcut to the Save button."

CommandMenu
- A menu in the menu bar that holds your own commands.
- Example: "Put the export actions in a CommandMenu."

hoverEffect
- A modifier that makes a view react when a pointer moves over it.
- Example: "Add hoverEffect so the button lifts under the pointer."
