📱 iOS Daily English - Day 159

TimelineView
- A view that updates its content on a schedule, such as every second.
- Example: "Wrap the clock in a TimelineView, so it ticks every second."

Drawing Group
- A modifier that draws a view and its children as one image with Metal, so complex drawing is faster.
- Example: "Add Drawing Group, because the particles are slow."

GraphicsContext
- The drawing tool inside a Canvas that you use to fill shapes, strokes and text.
- Example: "Call fill on the GraphicsContext to draw the face."
