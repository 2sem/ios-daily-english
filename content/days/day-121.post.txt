📱 iOS Daily English - Day 121

Accent Color
- The main color of your app that tints buttons, links and controls.
- Example: "Change the Accent Color to the new brand blue."

Named Color
- A color that you load from the asset catalog by its name.
- Example: "Load the brand as a Named Color, not as a hex value."

Color Set
- An asset catalog entry that holds one color with its light and dark versions.
- Example: "Create a Color Set so the card adapts to dark mode."
