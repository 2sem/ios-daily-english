📱 iOS Daily English - Day 105

Image View
- A view that shows a single image on the screen.
- Example: "Show the avatar in an Image View with round corners."

Content Mode
- A setting that decides how an image is scaled and placed inside its view.
- Example: "Use aspect fill as the Content Mode, so the photo has no gaps."

SF Symbol
- One of thousands of icons that Apple provides, which scale and match your text.
- Example: "Use an SF Symbol for the heart, no PNG needed."
