📱 iOS Daily English - Day 67

MutableCollection
- A collection protocol that lets you change items in place through the subscript, for example with `swapAt`.
- Example: "`Array` is a MutableCollection, so sort it in place instead of making a copy."

BidirectionalCollection
- A collection protocol that can step backward as well as forward, which gives you `last` and `reversed`.
- Example: "Make it generic over BidirectionalCollection, because we need `last`."

RandomAccessCollection
- A collection protocol that promises jumping to any index, or measuring the distance, takes one step.
- Example: "`String` isn't a RandomAccessCollection, so jumping to the middle is slow."
