📱 iOS Daily English - Day 266

Data Race
- A bug where two threads touch the same memory at once and at least one of them writes.
- Example: "A data race can crash the app."

Sendable
- A protocol that marks a type as safe to pass between concurrent tasks.
- Example: "Make the model Sendable before you pass it."

Isolation Boundary
- The line between two actors or tasks, where values must be safe to share before they cross.
- Example: "Passing a class across an isolation boundary needs care."
