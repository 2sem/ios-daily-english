📱 iOS Daily English - Day 120

App Icon
- The picture that represents your app on the Home Screen and in the App Store.
- Example: "Designers sent the new App Icon this morning."

Asset Catalog
- A folder in Xcode that stores your images, colors and icons.
- Example: "Put the new logo in the Asset Catalog, not in the project folder."

Image Set
- One named entry that holds the same picture in several sizes, such as 1x, 2x and 3x.
- Example: "Make an Image Set so the picture stays sharp on every device."
