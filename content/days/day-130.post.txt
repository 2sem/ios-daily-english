📱 iOS Daily English - Day 130

Status Bar
- The strip at the top of the screen that shows the time, battery and signal.
- Example: "Make the Status Bar light, so it is readable on the dark header."

Bar Button Item
- A button that you place in a navigation bar or toolbar.
- Example: "Add a Bar Button Item for creating a new note."

Large Title
- A big title at the top of a screen that shrinks when the user scrolls.
- Example: "Turn on the Large Title for the main list."
