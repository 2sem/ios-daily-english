📱 iOS Daily English - Day 136

Toggle
- A switch that the user turns on or off.
- Example: "Add a Toggle for the sound setting."

Slider
- A control that lets the user pick a number by dragging.
- Example: "Use a Slider for the volume."

Picker
- A control that lets the user choose one option from a list.
- Example: "Use a Picker to choose Easy or Hard."
