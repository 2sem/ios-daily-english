📱 iOS Daily English - Day 153

Scroll Indicator
- The thin bar that appears at the edge of a scroll view while the user scrolls.
- Example: "Hide the Scroll Indicator, because the design has no bar."

ScrollViewReader
- A container that gives you a proxy, so you can scroll to any view that has an id.
- Example: "Wrap the list in a ScrollViewReader to build a jump to top button."

Scroll Position
- A modifier that binds the id of the visible view, so you can read and change where the scroll view is.
- Example: "Save the Scroll Position, so the feed opens where the user stopped."
