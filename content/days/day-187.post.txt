📱 iOS Daily English - Day 187

gridCellColumns
- A modifier that makes a cell cover more than one column.
- Example: "Use gridCellColumns to make the title cover both columns."

gridColumnAlignment
- A modifier that sets the alignment for a whole column of a Grid.
- Example: "Add gridColumnAlignment to one name cell."

gridCellAnchor
- A modifier that sets where a view sits inside its cell.
- Example: "Use gridCellAnchor to put the icon at the top."
