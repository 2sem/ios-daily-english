📱 iOS Daily English - Day 276

Duration
- A length of time, such as seconds or milliseconds, stored as a precise value.
- Example: "Use a Duration instead of a plain number."

Clock
- A protocol for something that tells the time and can wait until a given moment.
- Example: "Pass a Clock into the retry helper."

ContinuousClock
- A clock that keeps counting even while the device sleeps, handy for measuring elapsed time.
- Example: "Measure the request with a ContinuousClock."
