📱 iOS Daily English - Day 158

Overlay
- A modifier that puts another view in front of a view, at the same size.
- Example: "Add the badge as an Overlay on the avatar."

Clip Shape
- A modifier that cuts a view to the outline of a shape.
- Example: "Use Clip Shape with a circle for the avatar."

Mask
- A modifier that shows a view only where another view is not transparent.
- Example: "Use a Mask, so the gradient shows only inside the letters."
