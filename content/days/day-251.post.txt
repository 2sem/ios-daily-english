📱 iOS Daily English - Day 251

Grand Central Dispatch
- A system library that runs your work on queues and manages the threads for you.
- Example: "Grand Central Dispatch manages the threads for us."

Serial Queue
- A queue that runs one task at a time, in the order the tasks were added.
- Example: "Use a serial queue to keep the order."

Concurrent Queue
- A queue that can start several tasks at the same time without waiting for earlier ones.
- Example: "A concurrent queue is great for heavy work."
