📱 iOS Daily English - Day 252

Global Queue
- A queue that the system provides and shares with every app, ready to use.
- Example: "Run the heavy work on a global queue."

Asynchronous Call
- A call that returns right away and lets the work finish later.
- Example: "An asynchronous call does not block the screen."

Synchronous Call
- A call that makes the caller wait until the work is finished.
- Example: "A synchronous call blocks the caller."
