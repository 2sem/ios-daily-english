📱 iOS Daily English - Day 100

Frame
- The position and size of a view, measured in its parent's coordinates.
- Example: "Print the Frame to see where the view really is."

Bounds
- A view's own position and size, measured in its own coordinates, so the origin is usually zero.
- Example: "Use the Bounds to draw inside the view, not the parent's space."

Transform
- A value that rotates, scales or moves a view without changing its layout.
- Example: "Rotate the arrow with a Transform, the layout stays the same."
