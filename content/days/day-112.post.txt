📱 iOS Daily English - Day 112

Interface Style
- The light or dark look that the screen uses.
- Example: "Set the Interface Style to dark only for the video player."

Dynamic Color
- A color that switches between its light and dark versions by itself.
- Example: "Use a Dynamic Color for the card, so it looks good at night."

Trait Collection
- A set of facts about the screen, such as light or dark mode and size, that a view can read.
- Example: "Check the Trait Collection before you pick the image."
