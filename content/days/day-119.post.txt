📱 iOS Daily English - Day 119

Info.plist
- A settings file in your app that stores its name, version and other basic information.
- Example: "Open the Info.plist and check the app name."

Display Name
- The app name shown under its icon on the Home Screen.
- Example: "The Display Name is too long, so iOS cuts it off."

Bundle Version
- The build number of your app, which must go up with every App Store upload.
- Example: "Increase the Bundle Version, or App Store Connect will reject the build."
