📱 iOS Daily English - Day 170

App Protocol
- A protocol for the main type of a SwiftUI app, marked with @main, that describes its scenes.
- Example: "The App Protocol is where the app starts."

WindowGroup
- A scene that shows your root view in a window, and can open more windows on iPad and Mac.
- Example: "Put the main screen inside a WindowGroup."

ScenePhase
- An environment value that tells you if the scene is active, inactive or in the background.
- Example: "Watch ScenePhase, and save the draft in the background."
