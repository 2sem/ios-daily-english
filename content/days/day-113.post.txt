📱 iOS Daily English - Day 113

VoiceOver
- A built-in screen reader that speaks what is on the screen.
- Example: "Turn on VoiceOver and test the whole checkout flow."

Accessibility Label
- A short text that VoiceOver reads to name a control.
- Example: "The icon button needs an Accessibility Label, or VoiceOver only says button."

Accessibility Hint
- An extra sentence that explains what happens after the user taps.
- Example: "Add an Accessibility Hint that says what the tap does."
