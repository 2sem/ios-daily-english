📱 iOS Daily English - Day 131

View
- A piece of the screen that you describe in SwiftUI.
- Example: "Create a new View for the profile header."

Body
- The property where a SwiftUI struct describes what it shows.
- Example: "Keep the Body short, and move extra code into small views."

Modifier
- A method that changes how something on screen looks and returns a new view.
- Example: "Add a Modifier to make the text bold."
