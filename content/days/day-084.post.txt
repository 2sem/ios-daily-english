📱 iOS Daily English - Day 84

Auto Layout
- The UIKit system that sizes and places views from rules you give it, instead of fixed frames.
- Example: "Use Auto Layout here, the card has to fit every iPhone size."

Constraint
- One rule about a view's size or position, like keeping its left edge 16 points from its parent.
- Example: "Add a Constraint for the height, the banner view is collapsing to zero."

Layout Anchor
- A property such as `leadingAnchor` that builds a rule for one edge or size of a view in a short, type-safe way.
- Example: "Use a Layout Anchor instead of the old initializer, it's much easier to read."
