📱 iOS Daily English - Day 108

Tab Bar Controller
- A container that shows a bar of tabs at the bottom and switches between screens.
- Example: "Use a Tab Bar Controller, with Home, Search and Profile."

Tab Bar Item
- The icon and title of one tab in the tab bar.
- Example: "Set the Tab Bar Item for each screen, with an SF Symbol."

Badge
- A small red number or dot on an icon that shows something new.
- Example: "Show a Badge with the unread count on the Inbox tab."
