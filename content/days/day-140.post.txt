📱 iOS Daily English - Day 140

Color Scheme
- The setting that tells whether the app looks light or dark.
- Example: "Check the Color Scheme before you pick the card color."

Dismiss Action
- An action that closes the current sheet or screen.
- Example: "Call the Dismiss Action when the user taps Close."

Environment Value
- A piece of shared information that SwiftUI keeps for every view, such as the locale.
- Example: "Read the font size from an Environment Value."
