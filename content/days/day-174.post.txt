📱 iOS Daily English - Day 174

Sidebar
- A column on the side that lists places or items to choose from.
- Example: "Show the folders in the Sidebar."

NavigationSplitView
- A view that shows two or three columns and updates the detail when you pick an item.
- Example: "Use a NavigationSplitView for the mail app on iPad."

Inspector
- A side panel that shows details about the selected item.
- Example: "Open an Inspector to show the photo size."
