📱 iOS Daily English - Day 147

Tap Gesture
- A gesture that runs code when the user taps a view.
- Example: "Add a Tap Gesture to the photo."

Drag Gesture
- A gesture that tracks the finger while it moves across a view.
- Example: "Use a Drag Gesture to move the card."

Magnify Gesture
- A gesture that tracks two fingers moving apart or together, to zoom.
- Example: "Use a Magnify Gesture to zoom the photo."
