📱 iOS Daily English - Day 40

Compound Assignment Operator
- An operator, such as plus-equals, that does math on a variable and stores the result back in it.
- Example: "Just use a Compound Assignment Operator: `count += 1`."

Operator Overloading
- A feature that gives an existing operator, such as plus, a new meaning for your own type.
- Example: "With Operator Overloading, we can add two `Point` values with a plus sign."

Custom Operator
- A feature that lets you declare a brand-new operator symbol and decide what it does.
- Example: "Please don't add a Custom Operator here, a named method is easier to read."
