📱 iOS Daily English - Day 268

Child Task
- A task that belongs to a parent task and ends before its parent does.
- Example: "A child task ends before its parent."

Unstructured Task
- A task made with `Task { }` that is not tied to a parent scope but inherits the actor and priority of its creator.
- Example: "An unstructured task needs manual cancellation."

Detached Task
- A task that inherits nothing from its creator: not the actor, the priority or the task-local values.
- Example: "A detached task ignores the current actor."
