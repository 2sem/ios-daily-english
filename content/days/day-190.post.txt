📱 iOS Daily English - Day 190

redacted
- A modifier that hides the real content and shows gray placeholder blocks while data loads.
- Example: "Use redacted while the list is loading."

AccessibilityFocusState
- A property wrapper that moves the VoiceOver focus to the view you choose.
- Example: "Use AccessibilityFocusState to focus the error message."

UIApplicationDelegateAdaptor
- A property wrapper that connects a UIKit app delegate to a SwiftUI app.
- Example: "Add a UIApplicationDelegateAdaptor for the push setup."
