📱 iOS Daily English - Day 164

ShareLink
- A control that opens the share sheet, so the user can send a link, text or image.
- Example: "Add a ShareLink to the quote screen."

PhotosPicker
- A control that lets the user choose photos from their library.
- Example: "Use a PhotosPicker for the profile photo."

Transferable
- A protocol that tells SwiftUI how to convert your type for sharing, copying or loading.
- Example: "Make the Note Transferable, so we can share it."
