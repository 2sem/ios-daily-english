📱 iOS Daily English - Day 95

Widget
- A small view of your app's content on the Home Screen or Lock Screen, built with WidgetKit.
- Example: "The Widget shows today's step count, tapping it opens the app."

App Extension
- A separate small program inside your app that adds a feature to the system, like a widget or a share sheet action.
- Example: "The share sheet item is an App Extension, so it has its own memory limit."

App Group
- A shared container that lets your app and its extensions read and write the same data.
- Example: "Put the database in the App Group, so the widget can read it."
