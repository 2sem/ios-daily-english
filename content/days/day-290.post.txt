📱 iOS Daily English - Day 290

Data Race Report
- A message from a sanitizer that names two unsynchronized accesses to the same memory.
- Example: "Read the data race report from the top."

Thread Sanitizer
- An Xcode diagnostic that watches memory while the app runs and reports data races.
- Example: "Turn on Thread Sanitizer in the scheme."

Concurrency Instrument
- An Instruments template that shows tasks and actors and their waits on a timeline.
- Example: "Open the concurrency instrument to see the slow task."
