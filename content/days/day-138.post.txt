📱 iOS Daily English - Day 138

Form
- A container that groups controls for a settings or input screen.
- Example: "Put the switches in a Form."

LazyVStack
- A vertical stack that creates its views only when they scroll into view.
- Example: "Use a LazyVStack for the long feed."

LazyVGrid
- A grid that fills columns with views and creates them only when they scroll into view.
- Example: "Show the photos in a LazyVGrid with two columns."
