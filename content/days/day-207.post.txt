📱 iOS Daily English - Day 207

Timeout
- The longest time a request may wait before the app gives up.
- Example: "Set a timeout of ten seconds for this request."

Retry
- The step of sending a failed request again, often after a short wait.
- Example: "Add a retry for the weak Wi-Fi case."

URLError
- The error type for network problems, like no internet or a lost connection.
- Example: "Catch the URLError and show a friendly message."
