📱 iOS Daily English - Day 132

VStack
- A container that places views in a vertical line, from top to bottom.
- Example: "Put the title and the subtitle in a VStack."

HStack
- A container that places views in a horizontal line, from left to right.
- Example: "Use an HStack for the icon and the label."

ZStack
- A container that layers views on top of each other, from back to front.
- Example: "Use a ZStack to put the badge on top of the photo."
