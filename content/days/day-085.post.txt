📱 iOS Daily English - Day 85

Intrinsic Content Size
- The natural size a view wants from its own content, like the width of a label's text.
- Example: "A label has an Intrinsic Content Size, so it only needs a position, not a width."

Content Hugging
- A priority for how strongly a view refuses to grow bigger than its natural content size.
- Example: "Raise the badge's Content Hugging, so the title label takes the extra space."

Compression Resistance
- A priority for how strongly a view refuses to shrink smaller than its natural content size.
- Example: "Bump the price label's Compression Resistance, so the name gets cut instead."
