📱 iOS Daily English - Day 116

Background Modes
- A capability setting that lets your app keep working in the background, for example to play audio.
- Example: "Turn on Background Modes so the music keeps playing."

Background Task
- A short extra amount of time that iOS gives your app to finish work after the user leaves.
- Example: "Start a Background Task so the upload can finish."

Background Refresh
- A system feature that wakes your app now and then to fetch new data.
- Example: "Background Refresh keeps the feed fresh before the user opens the app."
