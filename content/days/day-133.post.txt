📱 iOS Daily English - Day 133

Spacer
- A flexible view that pushes other views apart by taking all the free space.
- Example: "Add a Spacer to push the Edit button to the right."

Padding
- The extra space that you add around a view.
- Example: "Add some Padding, so the text does not touch the edge."

Alignment
- The rule that decides how views line up inside a stack.
- Example: "Change the Alignment to leading, so the text starts on the left."
