📱 iOS Daily English - Day 124

Long Press
- A gesture where the user holds a finger on the screen for a moment.
- Example: "A Long Press on the photo opens the preview."

Swipe Action
- A button that appears when the user swipes a table row sideways.
- Example: "Add a Swipe Action to delete a note."

Context Menu
- A small menu of actions that appears after a long press on an item.
- Example: "Put copy and share in a Context Menu."
