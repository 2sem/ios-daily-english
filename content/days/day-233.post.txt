📱 iOS Daily English - Day 233

Production
- The live version of your backend and app that real users use every day.
- Example: "Never test a risky change on production."

Staging
- A test copy of the live setup, where the team checks changes before real users see them.
- Example: "The new login works on staging."

Base URL
- The first part of every web address your app calls, shared by all of its requests.
- Example: "The base URL has a typo."
